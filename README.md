## 📊 Dashboard Features  

### **1️⃣ KPIs for Total Miles, Revenue, and Cost**
This section highlights the most important transport KPIs:
- **Total Miles**
- **Total Revenue**
- **Total Cost**

These KPIs give an instant overview of overall transport performance.

---

### **2️⃣ Revenue vs Miles by Shipping State (Trend)**  
A trend chart showing:
- How revenue changes with total miles  
- Comparison across different shipping states  

---

### **3️⃣ Revenue vs Miles by Shipping City (Map View)**  
A geographic map showing:
- Revenue contribution by shipping cities  
- Miles covered for each city  

Useful for analyzing **regional transport performance**.

---

### **4️⃣ Total Trips vs Trips by Trip Types**  
A comparison chart showing:
- Total number of trips  
- Distribution by trip type (Local, Long-haul, Express, etc.)

---

### **5️⃣ Percentage of Trips by Shipping State**  
A visual displaying:
- Trip distribution across states (in %)  

---

### **6️⃣ Revenue Miles vs Total Miles**
This visual compares:
- Total Miles  
- Revenue-Generating Miles

## 🎯 Project Objective  
The objective of this dashboard is to provide insights into:
- Revenue trends  
- Miles covered across regions  
- Trip distribution  
- Transport efficiency  
- Geographic performance  

This supports better **strategic decision-making** and **operational optimization**.
